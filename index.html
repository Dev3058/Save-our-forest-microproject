Why Forest Awareness Matters

Forests are called the "lungs of the Earth" because they make oxygen and absorb carbon dioxide. Every year, millions of hectares of forest are cut down.

Deforestation does more than remove trees. It disrupts entire ecosystems, changes local rainfall patterns, and leaves soil exposed to erosion. This can lead to landslides and make land less fertile for farming nearby.

Trees also help fight climate change by storing carbon in their trunks, roots, and the soil around them. When forests are cut down or burned, that stored carbon is released back into the air, adding to global warming.

Forests are also home to millions of people who rely on them for food, medicine, and a way of life. Protecting forests means protecting these communities as much as the trees themselves.

When people learn about forests, they make better choices that help protect them. Every small action adds up!
